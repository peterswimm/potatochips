# Code of Conduct

We commit to a respectful, constructive project space. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, and bad-faith attribution or license claims are not
acceptable.

Assume good faith, discuss the work rather than the person, and make room for
corrections. Credit upstream authors and contributors accurately. Respect
consent when sharing recordings, personal data, security reports, or AI prompts.

Report concerns privately through the channel in `SECURITY.md` or, for
non-security conduct concerns, through a GitHub issue requesting steward
contact. The steward may warn, moderate, remove content, or restrict
participation as needed to protect participants and the project.
